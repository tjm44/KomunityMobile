## 🎥 Recording & Device Setup Checklist

1. **Clean Screen & Status Bar:**
   - On Android/iOS emulator or physical phone, enable **Demo Mode / Clean Status Bar** (forces 9:41 AM, 100% battery, full Wi-Fi signal, hides random push notifications).
   - Enable **"Show Touches / Pointer Location"** in Developer Options so taps and swipe gestures are visible to the audience.
2. **Smooth Swiping Pace:**
   - Avoid quick or jerky thumb flicks. Use steady, continuous 1-second drag scrolls.
   - Pause for 1–2 seconds on important screens before navigating away.
3. **Data Prep:**
   - Populate clean mock data (realistic avatars, recognizable group names like *"Highland Savings Club"*, *"Bereavement Support Network"*).

## ✂️ Video Editing & Post-Production Guidelines

- **Framing & Background (for 16:9):**
  - Place phone screen inside an iPhone 15/16 Pro or sleek Android device mockup.
  - Add a soft dark gradient background (`#0B0F19` to `#1E293B`) with a subtle floating drop shadow.
- **Dynamic Zooms & Pan (Ken Burns):**
  - Punch in by 20%–35% when focusing on key UI elements like the **Wallet balance**, **Deposit modal**, or **Group Progress indicator**.
- **Sound Design (SFX):**
  - **Button Taps:** Add subtle, soft UI pops / haptic click sounds.
  - **Screen Transitions:** Soft whoosh / slide sound effects.
  - **Background Music:** Upbeat, modern corporate lofi or ambient electronic track mixed at `-22dB` to `-26dB` under the voiceover.
- **Typography / Captions:**
  - Font: Clean sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, or Outfit).
  - High-contrast white text with brand color accent tags (Emerald Green / Cyan / Indigo).
